Water quality (水质处理)

Q = 0.01 × Acatchment × C （m³/h）
Arequired = max[Q ÷ (0.5 × K/1000), 2% × Acatchment]

Design intensity: 10 mm/h. K: mm/h, maximum 1000. Clogging factor: 0.5. Default impervious C: 0.95. (设计雨强 10 mm/h；K 最高 1000 mm/h；堵塞系数 0.5；不透水面径流系数默认 0.95。)

Hydrology · SMAF 1 / 2 (蓄滞水)

Vdet = A × [dpond + 0.30(dmedia + dtransition) + 0.35ddrain]
Vret = min[0.35Adstorage, A × 72 × Ksoil] + A × 3 × 0.003

Use m for depth and m/h for Ksoil. Retention target = impervious area × 0.005 m; enter the detention target. Required area is the maximum of footprint, detention and retention requirements. (深度使用 m，土壤渗透率使用 m/h；蓄水目标为不透水面积 × 0.005 m，滞水目标由用户输入；三项面积取最大值。)

Table 48 footprint ratios: SMAF 1 = 5%, SMAF 2 = 3.5%. Equal-area layers also cover the infiltration footprint check. Table 50 uses max(input soil rate, 2 mm/h); measurements below 2 need separate assessment. (占地比例分别为 5% / 3.5%，等面积分层同时检查底部入渗占地；土壤设计值取输入值与 2 mm/h 的较大者，实测低于 2 时必须另核实际入渗能力。)

Assumptions & limitations (模型假设与边界)

Void space: media/transition 30%, drainage/storage 35%. ET: 3 mm/day for 3 days. Storage below the underdrain is separate from detention; SMAF assumes no impermeable base liner. (介质及过渡层孔隙率 30%，排水及蓄水层 35%；蒸散发每天 3 mm，计 3 天；管底以下蓄水层单独计量，SMAF 假定底部无不透水衬膜。)

Organic mulch volume is not deducted under the GD01 convention; deduct inorganic mulch separately. Slopes, freeboard, climate change, pipes, peak flow and flood storage are excluded. Provide pretreatment above 50 m². (有机覆盖物不扣体积，无机材料需另扣；未计算边坡、自由高、气候变化、管道、峰值与防洪；超过 50 m² 应设预处理；示例不是审批结论。)
